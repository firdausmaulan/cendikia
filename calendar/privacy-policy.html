This Privacy Policy describes how our calendar application ("App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

The App allows you to store the following information locally on your device:

  • Marked Dates: Dates you mark with custom descriptions and repeat settings
  • Preferences: App settings including color preferences, background images, and auto-open calendar settings

Important: All this information is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store this data on our servers.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We do not directly collect any personal information. However, third-party services we use may collect certain information automatically:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics (crashes, performance data)
  • Advertising identifiers (for personalized ads)

2. Third-Party Services

2.1 Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information to provide personalized ads. This includes:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Usage data

For more information about how Google uses data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps

2.2 Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising by:

  • Visiting the Google Ads Settings
  • Using your device's advertising settings:
    • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

3. How We Use Information

The information stored locally on your device is used solely to:

  • Display your marked dates and reminders
  • Remember your app preferences (colors, background, settings)
  • Provide calendar functionality

We do not access, collect, or transmit this information from your device.

4. Data Storage and Security

All user data (marked dates and preferences) is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences and local storage mechanisms.

Important: Since data is stored locally:

  • Your data is NOT backed up to the cloud
  • If you uninstall the App, all your marked dates and settings will be deleted
  • Data is not synchronized across multiple devices

We implement reasonable security measures to protect data stored on your device, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Storage/Photos Access: To allow you to select background images from your device (only when you choose to change the background)
  • Internet Access: To display advertisements
  • Network State: To determine if ads can be loaded

These permissions are only used for the stated purposes and are optional (except internet access for ads).

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. The only data sharing occurs through:

  • Google AdMob: For advertising purposes as described in Section 2.1

We do not have access to your locally stored marked dates or preferences.

8. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: All your data is accessible within the App
  • Deletion: You can delete marked dates individually or clear all app data through your device settings
  • Modification: You can edit your marked dates and preferences at any time

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

10. International Data Transfers

Since all user data is stored locally on your device, no international data transfers occur. However, third-party services (Google AdMob) may transfer data internationally as part of their services.
